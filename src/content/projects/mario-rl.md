Two reinforcement learning methods, the same level, the same compute budget. We compared learning speed, stability and final distance for A3C and PPO over four random seeds and up to 3 million timesteps each, and wrote it up as a paper. Both reached the flag. PPO got further on average, A3C was noisier.
