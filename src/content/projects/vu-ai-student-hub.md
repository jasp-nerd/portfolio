## The problem

Every student in my programme was rebuilding the same study notes, hunting for old
practice material, and manually computing what they needed on the exam to pass. There
was no shared place for any of it.

## What I built

A community-driven study platform covering **all 35 courses** of the BSc AI at VU
Amsterdam: 515+ quiz questions with difficulty filtering and explanations, 176 practice
problems, 169 curated resources, 142 student-contributed study tips, 17 course
summaries, and a grade calculator for every course. An AI course assistant (OpenRouter)
answers course-specific questions, with KaTeX rendering for the math-heavy subjects.

All content is managed through typed TypeScript data files — no database, no CMS —
so contributing is a copy-paste template and a pull request. Built with React 19,
Next.js 16, TypeScript and Tailwind CSS 4.

## Outcome

875 visitors and 3,450+ page views in the first two weeks, with a 35% bounce rate and
around 200 daily visitors during exam week. Open source, actively maintained, and
growing through student contributions — the part I'm most proud of is that it keeps
getting better without me.
