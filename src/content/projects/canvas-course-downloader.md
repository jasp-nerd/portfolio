Canvas was about to take my course files away at the end of a semester, so I wrote an extension that grabs all of it: files, pages, assignments, discussions, grades, quizzes, even lecture videos with captions. It exports to offline HTML or Markdown, which turns out to be a great way to feed a course into NotebookLM or Claude.

I put it on the Chrome Web Store and forgot about it. Then the Canvas data breach in May sent a wave of students and teachers looking for backups, and it was already sitting there. Strangers started sending pull requests. It has a Featured badge now, 4.8 stars and 30 releases.
